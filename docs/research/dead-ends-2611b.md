## 3. Strip-and-tile PEEC as the router's cell quote (quick physics M2)

**Did not work** (killed by its pre-registered criteria, closed 2026-11-24); `tools/research/physics/quickphys/PLAN.md` M2, 2026-11-21 to 2026-11-24.

- **Tried:** `kae.em.strips` (new name `haslam.em.strips`): a geometry-exact PEEC of one hot-loop cell as the cell quote, behind `KAE_CELL_QUOTE=raster|strips` (default raster). Tracks as Hoer–Love bars of true width; pads as equipotential nodes; vias as tubes landed on plane tiles; planes and pours as tiles clipped to KiCad's fills; dense complex LU with bordered updates; work counted (`em_kernel`, `em_dense`, `em_border`, `em_pair`). Three builds: M2 (qp-c, uniform tiles), rework 1 (graded quadtree tiles with inductive landings, a size budget that refuses, a copper-join check, P0 ≤ min(0.5, h)), rework 2 (solve in a loop basis, about one unknown per tile instead of MNA's three; P0 = min(0.5, h) on each plane's own lattice; track joins by union-find). Validation against the 0.0625 mm raster window on human-layout cell pairs and on the 24 converter boards, plus FastHenry and closed forms.
- **Evidence** [C]: kill 1 (ordering agrees on ≥ 70 of 76 non-tie comparisons): 20 / 74, then 3 / 76, then 38 / 76 (2-layer cells 36 / 50; P0 ≤ 0.235 mm 2 / 26). Kill 2 (p90 |ratio − 1| ≤ 15 % on 19 cells): 8 cells quoted, p90 0.84; then 2 quoted (9 refused for budget, 8 copper-join lost); then 13 quoted, p90 L 0.26 / R 0.39, median ratio L 0.96 / R 0.86. Still refused in rework 2: 4-layer cells of 4,147-47,705 loop unknowns. Ground truth passed throughout: strips at P 0.25 within −2 … +0.2 % of FastHenry, exact on closed forms; the raster reads low on every case. Bound: L_coarse ≥ L_fine at every f > 0; R only at DC (−4.7 % at 10 MHz).
- **Why dead:** dense MNA/loop solves cannot hold a 4-layer near region at P0 ≤ h within a router-step budget, and R reads low; the A/B against the raster quote was never run. The raster window remains the cell quote (live line `quick-physics` keeps M1: typed quantities and counted field work).
- **Reopen if:** an adaptive near region from a coarse first solve keeps 4-layer cells under about 4,000 unknowns. Reusable pieces on the tag: the loop-basis solver (`LoopSystem`), graded 2:1 quadtree tiles with inductive landings, the copper-join check, union-find track joins (they fixed real builder gaps), the FastHenry fixture and validation scripts (`find_pairs.py`, `validate_pairs.py`, `validate_ratio.py`, `ground_truth.py`, `bound_vs_f.py`).
- **Code:** `archive/qp-c2` 4fd4c3676e2829b4327df5e968686f4f70e0ed60 (`kae/src/kae/em/strips.py`, `tools/research/physics/quickphys/PLAN.md` with all three result blocks, `docs/research/physics/quick-physics-design.md` §4-§5). ## 4. Re-splitting a source's pin currents with the copper in its path (cm-scale)

**Superseded** by the die-in-copper fix (not built); A/B run, not judged, 2026-11-23.

- **Tried:** `kae.excite.evaluate` took each source's pin currents from the circuit solved with ideal copper and forced them into the copper. `circuit.external_share` gives a source's pin share α and driven impedance; `circuit.copper_scale` re-splits it with the copper's layout share z_cu = J_aᵀ Z J_a as a series impedance z_cu / α² in the pins' path (exact for one path); sources at pads (α = 1) unchanged. Motivating case: gos2022 mb02a1-2's CH340 cores (one 100 nF capacitor 45 mm away on traces) read 2.4 kΩ "mutual" at 100 MHz (L 3.8 µH), 7.6 V cable common mode against 0.4 mV (u 19,000), robust total 1.1e12. Pre-registered A/B: 60 human layouts (`final60`), operating facts `compiled-0833f61`, pitch 0.25 mm, seed 0; controls (`dc_*`, `heat`, cells' `overshoot`) must move ≤ 1e-6.
- **Evidence** [C] (read 2026-11-25 from the host's run files, not by the plan's script): 48 of 60 boards with a nonzero base total; controls unchanged (dc_drop 184, dc_heat 54, dc_loss 54, heat 797, overshoot 69 harms, none moved); moved harms: cm 605 of 1,893, supply 315 of 788, victim 225 of 682 (median ratio 1.0 each); board robust totals: 25 of 48 moved > 1 %, median 0.998, five fell 100-1,000× (ahillbom 0.0009, leodj 0.0047), one rose 11.8× (alvarop chaac-ng); `implausible` flags 31 → 9; completeness 48 → 48.
- **Why dead:** the one-path re-split is not right where a source has several paths, and the 11.8× board was never explained; the proper fix puts each die and package inside the copper in `evaluate`, as `wideband` does (handoff note 2026-11-24 on the live branch `handoff-2611`, `docs/research/handoff-2026-11-24.md`).
- **Reopen if:** never as a re-split; build the die-in-copper model and rerun this plan's observables as its check.
- **Code:** `archive/cm-scale` d2e2623cadaa167d1581fba988f66c2a80555d7f (`kae/src/kae/excite/circuit.py` `external_share`, `copper_scale`; test `kae/tests/test_excite_copper_split.py`; `tools/research/cm-scale/PLAN.md`). Host: `/var/tmp/$USER/cmscale/ab/{base,new}/` (60 evaluations each).
