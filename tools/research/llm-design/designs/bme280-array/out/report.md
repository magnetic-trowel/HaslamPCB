## Brief

> Make a board with 16 BME 280s, a mux that would be required, level converters so it can work anywhere from 1.8 to 12 volts, some sort of voltage conversion as well. Make sure that the heating from the voltage conversion can't impact the sensors. And it should also be as efficient as possible. JLC assembly. (Follow-ups: microamp quiescent current is fine; design for BME280/BMP280, stock is coming.)
