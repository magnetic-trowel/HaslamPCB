## Brief

> Make the best solar to battery converter we can: highest efficiency, broadest range, most power in varied conditions. Battery: lead acid or 4S LiFePO4, 10-50 Ah, 12-14 V. Run by an ESP32 (MPPT sweeps, charge voltages, on/off), minimal self consumption, current sensing. Small board: panel max under 25 V and about 10 W; high efficiency even when the panel only gives 100-200 mW (charging a sub-1 W system with an over-provisioned panel). Charge voltage continuously settable for float and lithium (e.g. max charge 60/80/90 %). Coulomb counting, continuous or with intermittent sampling that behaves as continuous. JLC assembly, few extended parts. ESP32: DFRobot FireBeetle 2 ESP32-C6 (product 2771). The ESP32 and its sensors are the only load; generic sensor header. No hardware cold lockout comparator: the ESP32 enforces it.
