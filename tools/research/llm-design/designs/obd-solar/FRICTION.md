# obd-solar end-to-end run: friction log

Scope: every friction point, defect, token sink and missing tool met while taking the obd-solar brief (OBD-II plug-in
solar trickle charger with USB-C PD out) through the HaslamPCB pipeline from a fresh cloud container. Goal: smoother
process, lower risk of bad boards, less work for the LLM.
Keys: friction, e2e, obd-solar, onboarding, token cost, tooling gaps

Severity: **H** = can produce a bad board or blocks the pipeline; **M** = costs a lot of time/tokens; **L** = annoyance.
Tag `[tool]` = would be better as a command than as LLM work.
