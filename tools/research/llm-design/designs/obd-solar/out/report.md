## Brief

> Make a PCB that plugs into the OBD-2 port on a car. It should have a connection for a solar panel that it can use to trickle charge the car via the always-on 12V. The power input should be a solar panel 13-24v, 5-10W that goes into an efficient mppt system and goes into the battery. It should have reverse-current protection. It should do the correct charge, absorb, float thing that lead-acids want. Also there should be a USB-C out that allows 5V, 9V, and 12V outputs. It should have an almost zero standby loss.

## Assumptions

- **A-1** Vehicle 12 V system: 11.5-14.8 V operating, 24 V jump start for 2 min, load dump suppressed by the vehicle (centralised) to <= 35 V; SMBJ24A clamps the residue (VC 38.9 V < 45 V U301 abs max) (confirm: human)
- **A-2** Flooded or AGM 12 V starter battery; the CN3767's fixed 14.8 V / 13.55 V suit both (not gel, which wants <= 14.1 V) (confirm: human)
- **A-3** U401 firmware (USB PD source stack on the UCPD peripheral, stop mode with UCPD wake-up) is written separately; the hardware defaults to USB-C off (R411) and 5 V (R309/R310) (confirm: human)
